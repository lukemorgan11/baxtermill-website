01 · Welcome

A hidden gem by the river

Baxter Mill Caravan Park sits at the bottom of a mile-long lane, right beside the river, about nine miles from the market town of Welshpool.

The park is laid out across two main fields, giving everyone plenty of space and big open views of the surrounding hills.

Whether you're after a quiet weekend away, a base for walking and exploring, or a spot to cast a line and watch the world go by, you'll find a warm Welsh welcome here.

05 · Nearby

Things to do in the area

Welshpool & Llanfair Light Railway

Ride a heritage steam train through the Banwy valley from Llanfair Caereinion to Welshpool.

Powis Castle & Garden

A medieval castle with world-famous terraced gardens just outside Welshpool.

Lake Vyrnwy

A stunning Victorian reservoir with walking and cycling trails, an RSPB nature reserve and great views.

Montgomery Canal

Peaceful towpath walks, wildlife and boat trips from Welshpool.
