Welcome

A hidden gem by the river

Baxter Mill Caravan Park sits at the bottom of a mile-long lane, right beside the river, about nine miles from the market town of Welshpool. The park is laid out across two main fields, giving everyone plenty of space and big open views of the surrounding hills.

Whether you're after a quiet weekend away, a base for walking and exploring, or a spot to cast a line and watch the world go by, you'll find a warm Welsh welcome here.
